# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator (1890–1974), best known in the context of knowledge management for conceiving the [[concepts/memex]] in his 1945 essay *"As We May Think"* (published in *The Atlantic*).

## Relevance to this wiki

[[wiki/sources/llm-wiki-pattern]] cites Bush as the intellectual ancestor of the LLM Wiki pattern. His 1945 vision — a private, actively curated personal knowledge store where the connections between documents are as valuable as the documents themselves — anticipates the pattern almost exactly. The one thing Bush couldn't solve was the maintenance problem: building and keeping the associative trails current requires effort that humans don't sustain. The LLM closes that gap.

## Key contributions

- **Memex (1945)** — proposed associative, trail-based navigation through personal documents as an alternative to rigid classification schemes
- Framed the challenge of human knowledge as a **navigation and linkage problem**, not a storage problem — prescient given how the web developed
- Articulated the idea that a researcher's trails through the literature have independent value and should be shareable
